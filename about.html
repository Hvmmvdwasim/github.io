Three decades of trading spices the honest way

What started as a single stall in Empress Market is now a small production house serving homes and restaurants across Karachi — without losing the habits that made customers trust us in the first place.

How we started

A stall at Empress Market, a promise to never cut corners

In 1994, Nasreen Qureshi began selling hand-mixed masalas from a single stall in Karachi's Empress Market, grinding small batches every morning so nothing sat on the shelf for long. Word spread through home cooks first, then restaurant kitchens asking for larger sacks.

Today, Zeera & Co. runs a small processing facility in Landhi, works with 45 partner farms across Sindh and Balochistan, and still follows the same rule Nasreen started with: grind only what you can sell fresh.

What guides us

Three commitments we don't compromise on

◈

Fair to the farmer

We pay partner farms above market rate for consistent quality, and buy direct — no commission agents in between.

◈

Honest to the customer

Every blend lists its full ingredients. No starch fillers, no artificial colour, ever — and we mean it on the label.

◈

Kind to the land

We rotate sourcing across regions each season to avoid overburdening any one farm's soil.

Milestones

From a market stall to a production house

1994

The Empress Market stall opens

Nasreen Qureshi begins hand-mixing masalas for neighbourhood households.

2003

First retail counter

Zeera & Co. opens its first standalone shopfront in Saddar, still at the same location today.

2012

Restaurant supply begins

Bulk sacks and custom blend ratios introduced for Karachi's restaurant kitchens.

2020

Online ordering & delivery

Karachi-wide delivery launched, along with our gift hamper line for corporate clients.

2024

Tasting lab opens in Landhi

A small lab space for quality testing and private blend tastings for chefs.
